This is our logo. It is our signature and the
endorsement of our brand. It is the graphic

Reduced version

clarity are critical. It simplifies internal details while
preserving the core structure of the ARMD mark,
ensuring instant recognition in compact formats
such as favicons, app icons, social media avatars
and minimal layouts.

1. Logotype

Main version. Basic guideline

Reserve area
In order for the logo to retain its visual potential and
legibility, its reserve area must always be respected.
This is delimited by the first "M" of ARMD, creating
a safety perimeter around an "M" in size.
This space must never be modified or invaded by
adjacent elements, be they text, photographs or
other marks.

Minimum size
The logo must always be reproduced at a size that
allows its legibility. It is recommended that it never
be reproduced below the indicated sizes.

Print (width): 15 mm
Screen (width): 80 px

Introduction

ARMD's photography style focuses on real
professionals in contemporary business
environments, using clean compositions, soft light
and a neutral palette with bold blue accents to
convey clarity, confidence and intelligence.

The framing alternates between strong individual
portraits, dynamic collaboration scenes and
key partnership moments, always emphasizing
movement, purpose and spacious layouts that feel
orderly yet human. This creates a refined, modern
visual language that balances approachability
with strategic rigor, positioning ARMD as a trusted,
forwardthinking advisor.

· Human-centered
· Clean and contemporary composition
· Natural light and soft tones
· Business-casual aesthetics
· Narrative of progress and collaboration

6. Visual system

Target grid system

The target grid is a key compositional device that
divides the layout into four balanced fields, creating a
clear structure for organising content.

It subtly echoes the ARMD symbol and guides the
reader's eye between typographic blocks, images
and empty space. This cross-shaped framework
ensures consistency across applications while
allowing flexible, dynamic arrangements that adapt to
different formats and messages.

ARMD Brand Guidelines v1 - 2025                           22
6. Visual system

Target grid position

The target grid can move freely across the layout,
allowing to position it wherever it best supports the
composition.

It may sit behind an image, frame a headline or
highlight a key data point. This target turns the grid
into an active pointer that organises content while
directing attention to what matters most.

6. Visual system

Supertarget backgrounds

For more atmospheric applications, the Supertarget
layout uses an oversized version of the ARMD symbol
as a background graphic.

Applied in ARMD Deep Blue over Dark blue or ARMD
Grey over Off-white, it adds subtle texture and brand
presence to slides, covers and title pages without
competing with the main content. By cropping the
symbol at different scales and positions, designers
can create varied compositions that feel dynamic yet
unmistakably ARMD.
